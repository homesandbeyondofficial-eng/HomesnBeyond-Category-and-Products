Can a newborn sleep with all the pieces in this set?

Decorative bedding is not automatically suitable for an infant’s sleep space. Keep the sleep surface firm, flat and clear of loose bedding, quilts, pillows, bolsters and soft toys, and follow your paediatrician’s safe-sleep advice.
