Find the right baby bedding set

A baby bedding set can bring a nursery together, but the details matter. Check your cot mattress measurements, compare the fitted sheet dimensions, and read the product page to see exactly which pieces are included. Sets vary by design, so the product specifications are your best guide.

Quilted details and playful nursery designs

From animals and rainbows to soft, storybook-inspired motifs, nursery bedding adds a decorative touch to a baby’s room. Look for product-specific details such as hand quilting and embroidery in each description.

What to check before you buy

  • Mattress fit: compare the fitted sheet’s width, length and depth with your cot or crib mattress.
  • Fabric: read the composition and thread count on the product page.
  • Pieces included: confirm exactly what comes with the set.
  • Quilt details: check the filling material and GSM where provided.
  • Care: follow the washing and drying instructions for the fabric and decorative details.

A thoughtful gift for new parents

Coordinated baby bedding makes a lovely gift for parents preparing a nursery or welcoming a little one. If you’re buying for someone else, check whether they already have a cot or crib and, if possible, confirm the mattress size.
