## What should you check before buying a baby bedding set?

A little checking before you order can help you choose a set that works with your nursery.

- **Cot mattress size:** Compare the fitted sheet measurements with your mattress, including the depth.
- **Fabric composition:** Check whether the product specifies 100% cotton and note its thread count.
- **Set contents:** Confirm whether the listing includes a quilt, fitted sheet, bolsters and/or pillow.
- **Quilt and filling details:** Read the stated filling composition and GSM rather than assuming every set is the same.
- **Care instructions:** Follow the product-specific washing and drying guidance to look after the fabric and decorative work.

## Baby bedding sets in India: choosing for your home

Indian homes experience different temperatures and humidity levels through the year. Think about the room, season and how your family plans to use each item. A cotton fitted sheet can be a practical part of a cot setup, while a quilt should only be used in a way that is appropriate for your baby's age and safe-sleep guidance. Do not add loose quilts, pillows, bolsters or soft accessories to an infant's sleep space. For babies, follow current safe-sleep advice from your paediatrician and recognised child-health authorities.

## Frequently asked questions about baby bedding sets

### What is included in a baby bedding set?

The contents depend on the product. Selected Homes N Beyond baby crib sets list a quilt, one fitted sheet, two bolsters and one baby pillow. Always refer to the individual product's contents and size section before buying.

### Are Homes N Beyond baby bedding sets made from cotton?

Selected crib-set product pages specify 200 TC, 100% cotton fabric. Check the individual listing for the exact material composition of the design you are considering.

### What size baby bedding set should I buy?

Choose based on the cot or crib mattress you own. Compare the fitted sheet's width, length and depth with the mattress measurements. Do not rely on the product name alone to determine fit.

### Is a baby crib set the same as a newborn baby bed set?

These phrases are often used to describe coordinated bedding for a baby's cot or crib. However, the contents and sizing can differ between brands and products, so check the product specifications carefully.

### Can a baby sleep with a quilt, pillow or bolster?

A product being sold as baby bedding does not mean every component is suitable for an infant's sleep space. Follow safe-sleep guidance: keep the baby's sleep surface firm, flat and clear of loose bedding, pillows, bolsters and soft toys. Ask your paediatrician for advice about age-appropriate use.
