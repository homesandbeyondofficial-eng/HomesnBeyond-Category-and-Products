## Category content (place below the product grid)

### Find the right baby bedding set

A baby bedding set can help bring the nursery together, but the details matter when you shop. Check your cot mattress measurements, look at the fitted sheet dimensions, and read the product listing to see which pieces are included. Sets can vary, so the product specifications are the best guide before you buy.

### Cotton baby bedding for everyday nursery style

Some Homes N Beyond baby crib bedding designs feature 200 thread count (TC), 100% cotton fabric. Cotton is a familiar bedding choice for many families. Fabric composition and thread count can vary by product, so check the individual listing for exact details.

### What comes in a baby crib bedding set?

Selected sets may include coordinated pieces such as a quilt, fitted sheet, bolsters and baby pillow. The exact contents differ by design. Check the product page for the complete set list and measurements before ordering, rather than assuming every baby bedding set includes the same items.

### Quilted details and playful nursery designs

From animals and rainbows to soft, storybook-inspired motifs, nursery bedding can add a decorative touch to a baby's room. Look for product-specific details such as hand quilting, embroidery and fabric accents in the description to understand the design and finish.

### What to check before you buy baby bedding

- **Mattress fit:** Compare the fitted sheet's width, length and depth with your cot or crib mattress.
- **Fabric:** Read the composition and thread count stated on the product page.
- **Pieces included:** Confirm exactly what comes with the set.
- **Quilt details:** Check the filling material and GSM where provided.
- **Care:** Follow the washing and drying instructions for the fabric and decorative details.

### Baby bedding sets in India

When choosing bedding for an Indian home, consider the room's temperature, season and your family's routine. A fitted sheet is one part of the cot setup, while quilts and other accessories need to be used according to your baby's age and current safe-sleep guidance. For infant sleep, follow your paediatrician's advice and recognised safe-sleep recommendations. Keep the sleep space firm, flat and clear of loose bedding, pillows, bolsters and soft toys.

### A baby bedding set can make a thoughtful gift

Coordinated baby bedding can be a lovely gift for parents preparing a nursery or welcoming a little one. If you're buying for someone else, check whether they have a cot or crib already and, if possible, confirm its mattress size. A design that complements the nursery without being too specific can be a practical choice.
